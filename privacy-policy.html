Privacy Policy

By installing and using the application, you agree to this Privacy Policy and to the collection, use, and sharing of personal data described here. If you do not agree, please do not install or use the application.

This app is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

This Privacy Policy applies to personal data collected through our applications and services.

3. Information and Data We Collect From You

We may collect and process the following types of personal data and related information when you use our applications and services.

Data automatically collected from applications

This may include device type and model, manufacturer, operating system platform and version, general location information such as region, country, or city, application version, advertisement browsing timing, and gameplay status such as stage start, stage end, and gameplay operation information.

We may also collect IP address information and advertising identifiers, including IDFA on Apple devices and AAID on Android devices.

Information you provide when contacting us

This may include your name, email address, and any other information needed for us to provide support.

Data obtained from advertising partners

We may receive personal information from third-party advertising partners depending on the permissions you provide to those partners.

Special categories of personal data

We do not knowingly collect special categories of personal data, such as data revealing racial or ethnic origin, political opinions, religious or philosophical beliefs, trade union membership, genetic data, biometric data used for identification, health information, or data relating to sex life or sexual orientation. We also do not knowingly collect data relating to criminal convictions and offences in connection with our applications and services.

We may use your data and information for the following purposes.

We use personal data to distribute applications, provide gaming services, and improve those services. This can include collecting and recording information such as game progress, correcting glitches, improving stability, developing new services, and sending notifications about updates, support matters, and service management.

Security

We may use your information to improve the security and stability of our services, monitor suspicious or unexpected use, send security alerts, and help identify or prevent fraud in advertising campaigns.

We may use information to display advertisements for our games in applications operated by other providers, to show third-party ads in our own games, and to present cross-promotional content for our other releases.

We may also use personal data to better understand preferences, improve ad relevance, and contact users about promotions, events, and related news from us or our partners.

We use personal data to respond to questions and provide customer support.

We may use information where necessary to comply with legal obligations.
